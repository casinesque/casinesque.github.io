Visited China for like three weeks, was my first trip in Asia and it has been a blast! Shangai and Beijing alone (the size of this cities are something way more larger than the term "Huge" can stand) absolutely worth the trip, but the terracotta Army was simply amazing. The idea of having 8000+ sculptured warriors specifically purposed to protect the emperor from his latest trip is unbelievable but the most important thing is that each single warrior has been sculpted to be different from others, this because the army has been shaped following facials of all areas of his kingdom (looks like back in time they didn't know yet about IaC...)
![Terracotta Army](/images/Terracotta_Army.jpg)

People i've met was nice and warm and they tend to be friendly and welcoming, food was awesome, they experiment A LOT and for a curious person like me this is a sort of a kitchen playground.
The culture is extremely fascinating, they combine the hyper dynamicity of their economy with a relaxed and calm way of living and thinking. 
They are really capable of merging those two aspects of life and society.
